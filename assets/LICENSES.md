# Sound and music licences

The subject provides no sounds, so every sound and the music below are free assets from other authors. All of them are under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain): they can be used, changed and shipped without asking or crediting, but the authors are credited here anyway. The sprite sheets have their own credits in [`sprites/ASSETS.md`](sprites/ASSETS.md).

`client.md`'s sound table ([Presentation](../docs/modules/client.md#presentation)) says when each file plays.

| `explosion_small.wav` | `explosion_crunch_000.ogg` | [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), Kenney | Decoded from Ogg Vorbis to 16-bit PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz, mono | CC0 1.0 |

The files were taken from two CC0 mirrors of Kenney's packs on GitHub:
- **Sci-Fi Sounds:** [Boyquotes/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-for-godot](https://github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-for-godot), commit `c094967`, folder `addons/kenney sci-fi sounds/`.
- **Interface Sounds:** [Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds](https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds), commit `4596a49`, folder `addons/kenney_interface_sounds/`.

The Ogg files were decoded with Raylib's `LoadWave` and written with `ExportWave`, so the audio itself is unchanged.
